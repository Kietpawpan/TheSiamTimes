The Siam Times Pétanque

How to play
  • Tap or drag on the court to choose where your ball should stop, or use the sliders. The blue ring shows where your hand is aiming right now; the dotted arc shows the flight and the small white ring where the ball first lands.
  • Pick a style. Roll and Half lob land early and roll the rest, so bumps in the gravel can push them off line. High lob drops almost dead. Shoot lands right on the ring, hard, to knock a boule or the jack away.
  • Each end starts with the jack, which must stop 6 to 10 m from the circle and at least 50 cm from the side boards. The side that threw it plays the first boule.
  • After that, the side that is not holding the point keeps playing until it takes the point or runs out of boules.
  • When all six boules are played, the side with the closest boule scores one point for each boule nearer the jack than the other side's best. The winner of the end throws the next jack. First to 13 wins.
  • A ball that touches the boards is dead. If the jack goes dead while both sides still have boules, the end is void; if only one side has boules left, it scores one point for each.
