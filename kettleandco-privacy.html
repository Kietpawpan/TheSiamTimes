Privacy Policy

Kettle & Co. for iPhone and iPad · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Kettle & Co. is a game you play on your own device. It doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

No accounts, no tracking

The game has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Your game stays on your device

Your progress is saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only learns which items you own, so it can unlock them.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.
