Privacy Policy

The Siam Times app for iPhone and iPad · Last updated: 23 October 2026

The short version

The Siam Times app does not collect, store or share any personal information about you. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

What the app keeps on your device

Articles you save with the bookmark button are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent to us or anyone else. To delete them, remove them from the Saved tab or delete the app.

The app also keeps a temporary copy of pages and the section list so they can be shown when you are offline. This copy stays on your device.

Loading the website

The app shows pages from thesiamtimes.com. As with any website, the servers that deliver those pages, and the services the pages use (such as Google Fonts), receive standard technical information like your IP address in order to send the page to you. We do not use this information to identify or track you.

Links to other websites open in Safari, where those websites' own privacy policies apply.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
