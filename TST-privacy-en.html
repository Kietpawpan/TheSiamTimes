In the interest of plain dealing, The Siam Times informs its users how their information is handled.

What the System Processes

The system does not collect information that identifies users directly. It must receive two things: the text a user pastes into the input field, and the password that confirms the user's identity at login.

Transmission to Outside Parties

To revise and translate text efficiently, the system sends the user's text to the services of third parties. These are Google Apps Script, which acts as an intermediary in processing commands; Gemini, from Google, or Claude, from Anthropic, depending on the model the user has selected; and the Wikimedia Commons API, which supplies image information and copyright references.

Storage and Security

Submitted text is processed in real time, solely to produce the result shown on the screen. We do not record the original text or the result in any database. Even so, users should not submit sensitive personal information, financial information, or confidential government or corporate material.

Acceptance of This Policy

By entering the password, the user acknowledges this policy and consents to the transmission of text to the artificial intelligence providers named above.

Contact

A user with questions about how the system processes data may write directly to the system administrator and application developer at support@thesiamtimes.com.
