How to play
  • Set the horizontal and vertical angle with the sliders, or tap the wall to aim there. The blue ring shows where the dart is pointing.
  • Gravity pulls the dart down on its way to the board, so aim above your target. More power gives a faster, flatter flight that drops less.
  • The outer thin ring is a double, the inner thin ring is a treble. The green outer bull scores 25 and the red bull scores 50.
  • In 501 you count down from 501 and must finish on exactly zero with a double or the bull. Going below zero, or leaving 1, is a bust: the turn doesn't count.
  • After three darts, collect them from the board to start the next turn.
