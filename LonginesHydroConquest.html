Structural Engineering & Wrist Ergonomics

Generational Refinement: Correcting Iconic Criticisms

The shift from the outgoing L3.780.4 to the 2026 L3.779.4 addresses nearly every point of enthusiast feedback accumulated over the past decade. By pairing an ultra-thin 3.85mm ETA 2892-derived movement with a re-contoured mid-case and recessed planisphere caseback, Longines reduced physical case height to 11.70mm. The introduction of universal 20mm lug spacing, streamlined crown guards, a 120-click Ultra-Chron bezel mechanism, and high-gloss black lacquer dial furniture elevates the HydroConquest into a modern dress-diver platform.

Horological Architecture & Engineering

Calibre L888.5: The Silicon-Hearted Engine

Beating beneath the planisphere caseback is the Swatch Group-exclusive **Calibre L888.5** (ETA A31.L11). Derived from the ultra-slim ETA 2892-A2 platform (3.85mm thickness) rather than thicker ETA 2824 workhorses, the movement operates at a custom **3.5 Hz cadence (25,200 vph)**. This cadence preserves a smooth 7-beat-per-second sweep while delivering a **72-hour (3-day) power reserve**. Enhanced with a monocrystalline silicon hairspring, the caliber delivers 10x ISO 764 magnetic resistance (~600 Gauss) and is backed by a 5-year warranty.
