Help and contact

For Makruk (Thai chess) and Rush Battalion on iPhone and iPad.

Found a problem, have an idea or a question? Email CONTACT_EMAIL. Please include the game's name, your iPhone or iPad model and what happened.

Makruk: how do I learn the rules?

Tap "How to play" on the home screen. Tapping a piece also shows where it can move.

Makruk: how do I unlock harder levels?

Beat the computer at your current level. A win earns one star, winning without hints or undo earns another, and winning within 40 moves earns the third.

Rush Battalion: a level is too hard

Spend coins on upgrades in the menu (starting squad, fire rate, damage) and try again.
