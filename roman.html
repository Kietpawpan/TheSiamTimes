1. Type Thai normally. No dashes needed; the result appears as you type

2. Use space to separate words

3. If a word comes out wrong, add dashes (-) between its syllables as pronounced, e.g. ราด-ชะ-บุ-รี

2. Announcement of the Royal Institute on Romanization (1999)

4. United Nations Romanization Systems for Geographical Names (2013)
5. ISO 11940-2
Exceptions:
   - Sound 'จ' uses 'CH-' instead of 'C-'
   - Do not add prime (') before the sound 'อ'
